"You can't waterproof from the inside. Coatings always fail."

You're right about coatings. A film held on by grip can't hold back water pushing from behind. That's why the industry went to drains and pumps.

Take it back to first principles and that rule turns out to be about surface films, not about stopping water from the inside.

It never made it into the residential basement trade. That's the opening.

WetBond isn’t a coating that fights moisture. It’s water-reactive, engineered to go after it, reacting with the water inside the concrete and bonding into the concrete itself.

The full technical explanation

What a WetBond basement looks like for your crew.

1 day

To apply WetBond to a typical basement. Prep and repairs vary by job.

The job, step by step

  1. Assess the basement and take moisture readings.
  2. Open and fill cracks, joints and tie-rod holes.
  3. Prepare the concrete chemically, then pre-wet it so WetBond drives in behind the moisture.
  4. Mix the two-part material on site and roll or brush it on.
  5. Record readings and photos and register the job for warranty.

The kit

Rollers, brushes and trays, mixing buckets and stirrers, acid and wire brushes, a hose and sprayer, a wet/dry vac, tarps and lights, and a heater for cold-weather jobs. No specialty rig. Most of it is already on your truck.

What joining involves

Your opening order

Material for about ten basement jobs: WetBond Core plus crack and injection repair products. It ships in one order, straight to you.

Certification

Online training, hands-on instruction on a live job, a written exam, and review of your first documented jobs. Travel is your own.

The 10-year manufacturer warranty is built into the product price, so there's no per-job fee for it.

Questions contractors ask

Do I need to be certified to buy WetBond?

No. Applicators can buy and apply it with the written protocol. Certification is what lets you register jobs for the manufacturer warranty.

Who backs the warranty?

WetBond. You handle repair labor in the early years and we always supply the material. If a dealer is ever gone, we cover the homeowner ourselves.

What if I have a slow season?

You keep buying and installing.

Do I work under your name?

No. You operate under your own company name. Co-branded materials are optional and always secondary to your brand.

Do you send leads?

No. WetBond is a brand we market to homeowners.

Does it work on block, brick and stone?

Yes. The application rate is set by how porous the wall is, and you learn to set it in certification.

When is the next certification training?

The first certification training runs the third week of October 2026.
